OVERVIEW

What LiveHandle is

LiveHandle is a free web toolkit for exploring publicly available social-media profile signals and creator metrics. It helps people review public Instagram and TikTok account cues, recent viewership samples when providers return them, simple engagement math, content-idea heuristics, and public video link resolution. LiveHandle is not affiliated with Instagram, TikTok, Meta, ByteDance, or other social platforms.

MISSION

Purpose

The project aims to make public-data checks more transparent: show what fields are available, avoid inventing missing metrics, and explain limitations clearly — including that authenticity-style scores are analytical signals, not real-world identity verification.

TRUST

Transparency commitment

LiveHandle uses third-party public data providers. Results depend on what those providers expose. When engagement or play counts are missing, the UI shows N/A or omits charts instead of fabricating numbers.

What this page does not claim

LiveHandle does not claim a corporate office, employee headcount, awards, partnerships, or certified accuracy percentages. It is a practical toolkit for public signals — not a law-enforcement or identity document service.
