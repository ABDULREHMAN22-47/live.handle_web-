SIGNALS

What this TikTok account analyzer reviews

When providers return a public profile, LiveHandle uses followers, following, posts, bio, and — when available — derived engagement from aggregate like totals. That is TikTok profile analysis and account analysis from public stats, not official TikTok Analytics.

The authenticity-style score is a public-signal model. It cannot confirm who operates the account.

USE CASES

When to use a TikTok profile checker

Use this free TikTok analyzer to review a public account before outreach, compare creator signals, or understand whether profile basics look complete. It is designed for quick public account checks, not private dashboard reporting.

If your goal is video performance, open the TikTok Growth Analyzer for average views, best-post multipliers, and views per follower when public play counts exist. After a public lookup you can also open an AI TikTok profile analysis based on that snapshot — stored only in this browser.

ENGAGEMENT

Engagement on TikTok vs Instagram

TikTok often exposes enough aggregate hearts to estimate engagement. Instagram frequently does not, which is why the Instagram analyzer may show N/A while a TikTok profile shows a rate.

For a transparent hearts ÷ videos ÷ followers estimate you type yourself, use the TikTok Engagement Calculator.
