REPORT

What this TikTok views analyzer includes

Growth pulls a live public follower snapshot plus, when the posts feed returns play counts, a recent-video viewership sample: average views, median views, best-post multiplier, consistency cues, and views per follower.

That makes it a TikTok views analyzer for the latest public videos, not a lifetime growth tracker. Share Stats copies the on-screen snapshot — it does not create a permanent public report URL for every username.
