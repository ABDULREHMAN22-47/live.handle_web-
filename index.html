OVERVIEW

Free social media analytics tools for creators

LiveHandle is a free social media analytics toolkit for public profile analysis. Creators, agencies, and researchers can inspect Instagram and TikTok account signals, run engagement-rate calculations, review a recent TikTok view sample when play counts exist, explore content-gap ideas, or resolve a public video link.

These are social media analytics tools for public data only. Lookups may be cached briefly on the API to reduce provider load. Results are not a substitute for official platform insights or identity verification.

TOOLS

Instagram and TikTok analytics in one place

The Instagram profile analyzer maps public completeness, follower/following balance, bio quality, and posting patterns into an authenticity-style score. The TikTok profile analyzer does the same with public TikTok fields and, when aggregate likes exist, a derived engagement estimate.

For recent video performance, the TikTok growth analyzer shows a live follower snapshot plus average views, best-post multipliers, and views per follower from the latest public posts that include play counts. That is a recent sample, not lifetime follower history.

LIMITS

What LiveHandle does not invent

If Instagram does not expose public likes or comments, engagement is N/A. If a TikTok posts feed has no play counts, view charts are omitted. Trend scores are ideation helpers, not verified search-volume charts.

Authenticity scores summarize public-signal risk. They do not prove who operates an account.
